Vice City Trophy Tracker is an offline guide and checklist for every one of Grand Theft Auto VI's 55 PlayStation 5 trophies — 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze. Each trophy carries its official name, description and grade, plus a step-by-step guide written in plain words with a named source, so you can check exactly what to do, where to do it and when it can be missed. A three-step roadmap lays out the order to earn them in on the road to the platinum, and the one missable trophy is flagged up front so a single playthrough is enough. The tracker itself runs entirely in your browser: tick trophies off as you earn them, filter by grade, story or collectible, hide spoilers for the 28 hidden trophies until you are ready to see them, and export or import your progress as JSON. No account, no network, no Rockstar assets — just the list, the guides and your progress. Built with plain HTML/CSS/JS (`app/`, data in `app/data/`); `main.ts` hands the app and its screenshots in.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
```
